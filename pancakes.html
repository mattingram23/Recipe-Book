Recipe Story

Shared by Matthew Ingram. These pancakes are cooked on a buttered and oiled griddle, with a gently mixed batter that stays a little lumpy.

Ingredients

Dry ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda

Wet ingredients

  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
  • 1 large egg

For the griddle

  • A generous amount of butter and additional oil, as needed

Instructions

  1. Mix all the dry ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Mix all the wet ingredients in a separate bowl.
  3. Combine the wet and dry ingredients. Mix just until mostly incorporated; the batter will still be lumpy. Do not overmix.
  4. Preheat a griddle, then add a generous amount of butter and oil, allowing the butter to melt.
  5. Using a large serving spoon, scoop the batter onto the preheated griddle.
  6. Flip once bubbles start to appear through the pancakes, then cook the other side until golden and cooked through.

Recipe Summary

Keep the batter a little lumpy and avoid overmixing. Watch for bubbles to appear before flipping.
