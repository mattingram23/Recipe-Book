Mom's Cheesecake

Category
Dessert
Prep Time
25 min
Cook Time
10 min + chill
Servings
10-12

Recipe Story

This is the first recipe that came to mind when starting to add recipes to this site. The cheesecake is an absolute legend in our family. I have loved this dessert my entire life. To me, this is the epitome of a family recipe. It's rich and creamy from the cream cheese and the crumble crust gives it a nice crunch.

Ingredients

Crust

  • 1 stick of butter
  • 1 1/4 cups flour
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Filling

  • 1 large cream cheese (update with exact measurement)
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 large Cool Whip

Topping

  • 1 bag prepared strawberry glaze
  • 1 pound fresh strawberries, sliced
  • or
  • 1 can cherry pie filling

Instructions

  1. Crust: Combine ingredients in a baking pan using a fork to create a crumbly texture that is roughly blueberry sized.
  2. Bake in a preheated oven at 400°F for 10 minutes. Stir several times while baking. Remove when deep golden brown and allow to cool.
  3. Add mixture to the bottom of your serving dish, reserving one-third for topping.
  4. Filling: Mix ingredients together and spoon over cooled crust.
  5. Strawberry Topping: Mix glaze with sliced strawberries and spread over filling. Add reserved crust crumbles on top.
  6. Enjoy.

Recipe Summary

Though it is not a traditional cheesecake, it has achieved legendary status in our family. We are talking more than 40 years of loving this dessert. It has traditionally been served in a large baking dish, but it can be served in almost any vessel. Individual serving cups make a nice presentation and travel well. Though cherry and strawberry are most popular in our family, almost any berry topping works well.
